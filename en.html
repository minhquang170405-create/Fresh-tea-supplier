TS 567 MÃ DỌ SEA TEA · THE STORY OF OUR TEA

The essence of
Cù Mông's mountain forests

Some places do more than nurture tea plants; they give their tea a character found nowhere else.

On the Cù Mông mountain range in central Vietnam, TS 567 Mã Dọ Sea Tea grows where forested highlands meet the ocean breeze. Here, nature shapes a distinctive setting, and the tea follows the land's own rhythm.

We care for this tea with respect for its growing area and each harvest season. Selected shoots are hand-picked, reflecting the landscape's natural conditions and the dedication of the people who have worked with the tea for many years.

For us, TS 567's value springs from the character of the land that gives it life. This is a story of nature, of safeguarding a distinctive tea source, and of bringing the original qualities of Vietnamese tea closer to those who truly appreciate it.

TS 567 Mã Dọ Sea Tea — more than a cup of tea, it carries the imprint of Cù Mông's mountain forests in every leaf.

A HARVEST SHAPED BY NATURE

The value of rarity.

TS 567 Mã Dọ Sea Tea is not mass-produced. The harvest is approximately 20 kg of fresh tea leaves every two months; each picking remains subject to natural conditions and the tea plants' growth cycle.

That limited yield gives the tea its distinctiveness and reflects the philosophy we follow: respect for nature, care for the local tea landscape and preservation of its original character.

We pursue careful selection and the unique qualities of this landscape, rather than volume.
