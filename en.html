THE STORY OF OUR TEA

A journey
from mountain to sea.

TS 567 Mã Dọ Sea Tea comes from the Cù Mông mountains of central Vietnam. The tea plants grow amid forested mountains in a distinctive climate shaped by the meeting of upland air and the nearby sea.

We are honoured to introduce this rare regional tea, hand-picked with care and preserved through years of dedication.

EXCEPTIONALLY LIMITED HARVEST

Precious in its authenticity.

TS 567 Mã Dọ Sea Tea is not mass-produced. The harvest yields only approximately 20 kg of fresh tea leaves every two months.

This natural scarcity is part of the tea's distinct character. It also reflects our commitment to caring for the growing area, protecting nature and respecting its original qualities.
